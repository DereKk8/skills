---
name: grill-to-intent
description: Use when I want to turn a raw idea into an intent spec or a prompt for a strong model, refine an idea, create an AGI prompt, or grill a concept.
---
Skill version: 1.0.0

When I invoke /grill-to-intent on an idea, find out what only I know about it by asking at most one round of up to three short questions, each with your recommended answer and only about things that would change the result (which value wins when two collide, what excellent looks like and what would feel wrong, what works but is still unacceptable), skip the questions when my message already answers them or I say synthesize, ask one more question when two things I said conflict, even in a later answer, naming the conflict and asking which side wins (or, if I said synthesize, pick the side my priorities favor and say so under Priorities), treat a technology I require as a hard limit and one I only mention as a preference, invent nothing I did not ask for, and then reply with only a one-page plain markdown spec under the headings Outcome (what must be true and why), Priorities, Taste, Freedom and boundaries, and Proof of life (an exact entry point and numbered checks a stranger can run in under 30 seconds), never with code or files, even when my idea reads like a build request.
