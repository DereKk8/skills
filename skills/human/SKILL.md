---
name: human
description: Write the requested content in a natural, easy-to-read human style with no AI bloat. Load when the owner types /human <request>, or asks for something written like a human and not like an AI.
---
Skill version: 1.0.0

Do the request and write the result the way a thoughtful person would explain it to a busy colleague who has no context and reads on a phone: say the point first, keep only what the reader needs to understand or act, with the exact names, numbers, and caveats that matter, explain in plain words any term the reader may not know, use short sentences in active voice with common literal words, use a list or bold only where it helps the eye find parallel items or steps, and leave out filler, repetition, hedging, hype, jargon, stock phrases, and em dashes, so the whole reads shorter and easier than the usual AI answer.
